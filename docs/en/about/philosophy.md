# Name & Logo Philosophy

## What the name means

**WANTIMPRES-26** is a technical acronym that sums up what the platform does:

| Letter | Word | What it means for the platform |
|---|---|---|
| **W** | Workforce | Protects how employees work with AI |
| **A** | AI | Covers public GenAI apps, internal LLM apps, and agents |
| **N** | Network | Guards the data paths between users, apps, agents, and models |
| **T** | Threat | Detects threats such as prompt injection, jailbreaks, and data exfiltration |
| **I** | Inspection | Inspects prompts, files, responses, and tool calls |
| **M** | Monitoring | Watches posture, trends, and incidents continuously |
| **P** | Policy | One versioned policy that applies at every enforcement point |
| **R** | Risk | Scores the risk of every interaction from 0 to 100 |
| **E** | Enforcement | Carries out decisions: allow, warn, redact, hold, or block |
| **S** | System | One complete system, not a collection of separate tools |

**26** marks the **2026 edition**, the year the platform was designed. In code, policy, and deployment config the name is written `wantimpres26`.

::: info Not a political name
The name is a technical acronym of the platform's functions. It does not refer to any state institution and is not affiliated with any institution, party, or political figure.
:::

## What the logo means

![WANTIMPRES-26 logo](/logo.svg){.home-logo}

The logo shows a **security guard** standing inside a **shield**:

- **Navy shield.** Protection and trust. The company and its customers can rely on their data being guarded.
- **Guard's cap.** Vigilance. A guard is always on duty at the gate, just as the platform's enforcement points inspect every AI interaction.
- **Gold badges on the cap and chest.** Integrity and accountability. Every decision is recorded and answerable through the audit log.
- **Terracotta uniform.** The courage to stop what is dangerous, and the warmth of service. A guard's job is to help people work safely, not merely to say no.

A good guard does not shut the gate on everyone. They recognise who is coming, weigh what is being carried, and decide fairly. That is how WANTIMPRES-26 treats AI.
