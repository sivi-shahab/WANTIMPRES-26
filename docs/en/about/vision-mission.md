# Vision & Mission

::: tip Draft
This vision and mission are derived from §1 of the [Specification](../spec): vision, problem statement, and principles. Revise them before official use.
:::

## Vision

**To be the trusted guardian of every AI interaction in the company, so that AI can be adopted quickly without sacrificing data confidentiality or customer trust.**

## Mission

1. **See every AI in use.** Discover sanctioned AI apps, *shadow AI*, and autonomous agents, and give each one a clear status and owner.
2. **Protect data on every channel.** Recognise and secure customer data, credentials, and intellectual property in prompts, files, responses, tool calls, and stored data.
3. **Weigh before acting.** Run one policy across every enforcement point. Every decision is explainable, versioned, and can be simulated before it takes effect.
4. **Keep agents within bounds.** Give every agent an identity, minimum privileges, short-lived credentials, human approval for risky actions, and an emergency stop.
5. **Prove compliance.** Provide auditable evidence for Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law, POJK 11/2022, OJK's AI governance guide for banking, and ISO/IEC 42001.
6. **Respect privacy.** Store no raw content by default, pseudonymise analytics, and restrict access to forensic evidence.

## Our values

| Value | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Data-centric** | Policy follows the type and sensitivity of the data, not just the app's name. |
| **Decision separate from enforcement** | One decision point for every channel, so rules never fork. |
| **Defense in depth** | Deterministic rules, ML models, and architectural controls cover each other's gaps; no single detector is trusted alone. |
| **Privacy by default** | Only metadata, hashes, and redacted snippets are stored. |
| **Explainable decisions** | Every decision carries its reasons, policy version, and model version. |
| **Everything versioned** | Policies, models, and regulatory mappings can be traced and rolled back. |
| **Explicit failure modes** | Every enforcement point knows when to fail closed and when it may fail open with an alarm. |
